Scent Directory

'Smoky & Dark'

Smells like: A wood fire dying down, a leather jacket left near incense, or the inside of an old wooden chest.

Best for: Evening wear, autumn/winter, those who want presence in a room.
Notes in this family: Smoky, Leather-Forward, Tarry, and Black Oud.

'Woody & Earthy'

Smells like: Fresh-cut timber, damp soil after rain, the inside of a cedar wardrobe, or a forest floor.

Best for: Everyday wear, unisex appeal, those new to oud.
Notes in this family: Raw, Khmer, Hindi, and Cedarwood Oud.

'Sweet & Resinous'

Smells like: Warm honey, dried fruit, amber, or vanilla. Feels almost edible and rich, without being too sugary.

Best for: Cold evenings, Casual events, gifting to someone new to oud.
Notes in this family: Musk blends, Amber, Vanilla, and Malay/Borneo Oud.

'Floral & Soft'

Smells like: Rose petals warmed by the sun, a bouquet sitting in a warm room, but with a rich, woody base.

Best for: Daytime, office wear, those transitioning from mainstream Western perfumes.
Notes in this family: Persian-Style blends, Rose, Jasmine, Lily of the Valley, and Tuberose Ouds.

'Clean & Musky'

Smells like: Warm skin, fresh linen, imagine catching a faint trace of someone that just stepped out a steam room. Subtle and intimate.

Best for: Office, everyday wear, minimalists.
Notes in this family: Sandalwood, Citrus, Frankincense, and White Oud.

'Spicy & Warm'

Smells like: Cardamom, Saffron, or Black Pepper layered over a rich wood base. Like spiced tea sitting on a carved wooden table.

Best for: Evening, autumn/winter, confident wearers who want complexity.
Notes in this family: Tobacco, Cardamom, Nutmeg, Saffron, Cinnamon Oud.
